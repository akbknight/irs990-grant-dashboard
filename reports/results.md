# Results — IRS Form 990 Grant Analysis Dashboard

## Key Findings (2019–2024)

### Dataset Scale

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total grant records | 9,693,767 |
| Total grant dollars | $550.2B |
| Years covered | 2019–2024 |
| Jewish grants (count) | 275,446 |
| Jewish grant dollars | $10.6B |
| Jewish share (by count) | 2.84% |
| Jewish share (by dollars) | 1.93% |

### COVID-19 Impact (2020)

2020 shows elevated total grant activity relative to 2019 and 2021, driven by emergency relief grant-making by major foundations and donor-advised funds in response to COVID-19. Human Services and Health categories show the most pronounced spike. Jewish organizations also showed elevated emergency giving in 2020, primarily through federation emergency relief funds.

### Geographic Concentration

Jewish grant-making is heavily concentrated in:
1. **New York** — home to the largest Jewish community foundations and federation grant-making arms
2. **California** — major private foundations in Los Angeles and Bay Area
3. **Florida** — Florida has the third-largest Jewish population by state

These three states account for the majority of Jewish grant dollars by grantee state.

### Grant Size Distribution

The grant size distribution is heavily right-skewed:
- Median grant size: approximately $25,000
- Mean grant size: approximately $57,000 (pulled up by large foundation grants)
- ~60% of grant records are < $50,000
- < 1% of grants are > $1M, but these represent a disproportionate share of total dollars

### Top Grantmakers

The top 20 grantmakers by total dollars are dominated by:
- Large donor-advised fund sponsors (Fidelity Charitable, Schwab Charitable, National Christian Foundation)
- Major private foundations
- Community foundations in major metro areas

### Subject Distribution

By dollar volume across all grants:
1. Human Services (largest by count)
2. Education
3. Health
4. Religion
5. Public Benefit

Jewish grant-making over-indexes on Education and Religion relative to the overall distribution.

---

## Dashboard Validation

KPI figures were cross-checked against published Giving USA 2024 benchmarks and Foundation Center data. The $550B total over 6 years is consistent with foundation and organizational grant-making at scale. The Jewish totals are consistent with published estimates from the Jewish Federations of North America.
